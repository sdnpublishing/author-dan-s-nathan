About Dan S. Nathan

DS

Dan S. Nathan

Dan S. Nathan writes science fiction that collides hard philosophy with big speculation. His Project Nova series asks what happens when humanity achieves genuine transcendence — and discovers the answer is more complicated, stranger, and funnier than anyone expected.

Part hard SF, part philosophical comedy, part something entirely its own — Nathan's fiction is for readers who want their genre to think as hard as it entertains.

If you've ever wondered what you'd actually do if you became a god, this is your book.
